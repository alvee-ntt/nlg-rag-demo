# Underwriting Guide Test Questions (FlexLife)

Source: **Life Insurance Underwriting Guide, Cat No 62797(0126)**. Indexed as `From NLG/Underwriting Guide.pdf` (doc #59).
Page numbers are the ones printed on the guide; they match the PDF page numbers.

★ = most likely to trip up the RAG. #1–3 depend on the p.23 requirements grid, which extracts badly to text. #52 is a hallucination check. The ★ medical questions (61–120) depend on a checkmark column that text extraction drops entirely.

Note: `From NLG/Downloaded Files/Life-Insurance-Underwriting-Guide.pdf` (doc #51) is a different edition of the same guide, so answers citing it may differ.

## Specific medical conditions

"Best offer" answers come from the **Potential Best Offer** column on pp.35–37: Better than Standard / Standard / Substandard. They assume the condition is fully evaluated, well controlled and has no complications. Multiple conditions can change the offer.

★ **Retrieval warning:** that column is only green checkmarks in the PDF, and text extraction drops them. The indexed chunks have the condition and its criteria but **not** the rating class. A correct answer to a "best offer" question is unlikely to come from the RAG unless ingest is fixed; watch for the assistant making up a class.

### Autism, developmental conditions and communication

The guide never mentions non-verbal applicants. Its language rules (pp.3, 6, 7) assume a spoken language. Cerebral palsy has different age cutoffs for full underwriting (over 6, p.35) and RapidProtect (over 8, p.20).
